Privacy Policy

Updated 18 November 2026. This notice describes how darkfeel.com.ua handles information when you visit the site or contact Dark Feel.

Privacy choices

The site stores your privacy choices in your browser for up to 180 days. You can change or withdraw your choices at any time. The same controls are available in the site footer.

Website analytics

If you allow analytics, the site uses randomly generated browser and session IDs to measure visitors, visits and page views. The browser ID stays on this device until you clear it or turn off analytics. A visit session ends after 30 minutes of inactivity. Reports include the page path and the referring hostname or campaign source, when available. The analytics database does not store IP addresses, full referring URLs or full query strings. Analytics records are stored on Railway in the United States and used to measure site performance.

Spotify and YouTube

If you allow embedded media, Spotify and YouTube load on the site. These providers may receive information such as your IP address and browser details and may use cookies or similar storage. You can also open the media directly on their sites.

Spotify Privacy Policy · Google Privacy Policy

Advertising and email

Advertising tracking and a mailing list are not currently active on this site. When these features are introduced, advertising consent and email subscription will be separate choices.

Booking and privacy requests

If you send a booking enquiry, the address and information you include are used to respond to your request. For privacy questions or requests to access, correct or delete information, email book.darkfeel@gmail.com.
